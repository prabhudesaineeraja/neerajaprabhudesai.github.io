Built and Building

I work at the intersection of computational linguistics, language analysis, and clear technical communication. My interests include NLP, language technology, linguistic data, evaluation, and building user-centered systems that make complex ideas easier to understand.

Mystery Language Identification Used acoustic and phonetic analysis in Praat to identify an unknown language through spectrograms, formants, VOT, and IPA-based sound inventory building. Identified the language as Nivaclé through comparative linguistic analysis.
Language Bias in NLP Datasets Explored how linguistic bias enters NLP datasets through data collection, annotation, model design, and research framing. Focused on unequal language representation and its impact on low-resource languages.
Multi-Document Summarization with LexRank (ongoing) Developing a LexRank-based system for extractive summarization of clustered news articles. Using graph-based ranking to identify the most informative sentences across documents.
Phonetic Analysis of Sanskrit Sandhi (ongoing) Investigating the acoustic realization of Sanskrit sandhi in connected speech. Exploring how phonological rules surface as measurable phonetic variation.
